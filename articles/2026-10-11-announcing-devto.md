Hi dev.to 👋

**GwinG** is a language-exchange messenger we're building — chat, voice and
video calls, media messages, on-the-fly translation and transcription. The
client is Flutter, the backend is FastAPI + PostgreSQL, and there's a small
fleet of Redis, MinIO and AI services underneath.

From today we're changing how we work in the open: **every change we ship in
the app gets a short write-up here.**

## What that means

- ✍️ New features, fixes and refactors become a small post — what changed and *why*.
- 🧱 The interesting bits (a WebSocket failover, a media upload quirk, a Flutter
  widget that misbehaved at 2× text scale) get the detail they deserve.
- 🗺️ Over time it becomes a public changelog of the project.

No promises of a fixed schedule — just a commitment to stop keeping the
interesting parts to ourselves.

That's it for the first one. The next post will be about something we
actually broke and fixed. 🙂

— the GwinG team
