The app is now called **Folcadi**.

The old name was **GwinG**. Everything else is the same: your account, your conversations, your settings, and the people you talk to.

The new name is rolling out across the app, the website, and the desktop builds. If a notification or a window title still shows the old name, update to the latest build.

That's it.

Welcome to Folcadi.

— the Folcadi team
